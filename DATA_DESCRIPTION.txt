grid_0p1deg/
  Derived 0.1 degree by 0.1 degree grid products and summary statistics.
  These files contain no exact pumpjack coordinates.

SPATIAL REFERENCE

All longitude and latitude fields use WGS84 geographic coordinates. Grid products use a 0.1 degree by 0.1 degree analysis grid.

PUMPJACK CONFIDENTIALITY

Exact pumpjack locations are intentionally excluded. Pumpjack variables in grid_0p1deg are counts, densities, threshold classes, or aggregated candidate indicators only.

RAW DATA NOTES

EDGAR files: REF_TRF and PRO_FFF CO2 files cover 2000-2024. PRO_OIL and PRO_GAS CH4 files cover 2019-2024.
ODIAC files cover 2000-2024.
ERA5 wind files cover 2018-2025.
TROPOMI CH4 files are the downloaded observations used for the regional processing workflow.
Natural Earth files provide map geometry only.
